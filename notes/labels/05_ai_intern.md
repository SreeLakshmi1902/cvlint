
About the job

Kogan.com is a pioneer of Australian eCommerce. Our vision is to leverage and build technology to deliver personalised, innovating and fast customer experiences and whilst doing so being pragmatic with our solutions, scalable with our architecture, strong with our quality and not being afraid to fail quickly.

In this role, you'll work with our IT & AI Operations team to identify opportunities to automate repetitive tasks, build AI-powered tools and workflows, and improve how we work across Kogan.com. You'll gain hands-on experience with large language models (LLMs), AI agents and emerging technologies, turning ideas into practical solutions that deliver real impact.

What you'll do:

* AI Development: Build and test AI-powered tools, agents and automations to solve real business problems.
* Smart Automation: Work closely with our Systems Administrators to identify repetitive IT tasks and tickets, and develop automated workflows, shortcuts and AI-powered chatbots to resolve common issues.
* AI Integration: Support the integration of AI tools into existing systems and workflows to improve efficiency across the business.
* Prompt Engineering & Enablement: Help build and maintain internal prompt libraries, AI documentation and resources to support teams in using AI effectively and securely.
* Experimentation: Explore emerging AI technologies, LLMs and APIs, turning new ideas into practical solutions.
* Security & Responsible AI: Support secure AI development and deployment, with a focus on data privacy, cybersecurity and responsible AI practices.
* Testing & Troubleshooting: Test, troubleshoot and refine AI solutions to ensure they are reliable, secure and effective.
* Continuous Improvement: Bring fresh ideas, challenge existing processes and identify opportunities to work smarter through AI.

What you'll need:

* The Go-To Tech Person: You're the person your friends and family call when their tech breaks. You love building computers, messing around with networks, or digging into software settings.
* AI Enthusiast: You don't just use AI to write emails; you understand how LLMs work, you enjoy experimenting with prompt engineering, and you've played around with tools like Zapier, Make.com, Power Automate, or OpenAI APIs.
* Problem Solver & Analytical Thinker: You look at a repetitive problem and think, "How can I write a script or build an automation so I never have to do this manually again?"
* Keep it Real Communication: Excellent verbal and written communication. You can explain a technical solution to a non-technical person without using confusing jargon.
* Education: Currently studying or recently graduated with a degree/diploma in Information Technology, Computer Science, Data Analytics, or a related field.
